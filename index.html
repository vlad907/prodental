Invisalign Treatment

Straighten your teeth, clearly.

Invisalign treatment is the process of wearing a series of clear, removable aligners that gradually straighten your teeth through advanced digital 3D image technology based on your specific needs — closing gaps or spaces and correcting bites toward your desired position.

01

The Treatment

The journey begins with a scan or impressions. Your orthodontist works closely with Invisalign treatment specialists to develop a complete digital treatment plan, carefully plotting your best smile from start to finish.

02

The Aligners

You'll receive Invisalign clear aligners created with proprietary SmartTrack material. Wear them for 22 hours a day, but easily remove them so there are fewer interruptions to your lifestyle.

03

The Transformation

As you progress through treatment, you'll see your smile and life transform through a series of small, expertly designed shifts toward your final, desired smile.

Over Bite

An overbite is when your upper front teeth overlap with your lower front teeth. Most people have at least a little overbite.

Under Bite

An under bite is when you close your mouth and your lower front teeth are in front of your upper teeth.

Cross Bite

When you close your mouth and some of your upper teeth rest on top of the lower teeth rather than on the outside, like they normally would.

Gaps In Teeth

When you have empty spaces between two or more of your teeth, this is a spacing issue or gap teeth.

Open Bite

When the mouth is closed and your upper and lower teeth don't touch, this is referred to as an open bite.

Crowded Teeth

When there is not enough space in your jaw for your teeth to fit normally, they can bunch up, overlap and twist.

How long does it take to straighten teeth with Invisalign?

The average time needed for Invisalign to straighten your teeth is about 12 months. The length of treatment depends on the severity of your case and can only be determined by your doctor.

How will Invisalign effectively move my teeth?

Invisalign doesn't use metal brackets or wires to move your teeth. Instead, a series of clear aligners apply constant pressure to your teeth. During each stage, only certain teeth are allowed to move. Many people start seeing a difference in their teeth within two to three months of starting to use the aligners.

What can you not eat with Invisalign?

With the removable nature of the aligners, you can eat and drink whatever you want while in treatment. Brush your teeth after each meal and prior to re-inserting your aligners to maintain proper hygiene and fresh breath.

Will smoking stain the aligners?

We discourage smoking, or drinking tea or coffee, while wearing aligners, because it is possible for the aligners to become discolored.

How often must I see my Invisalign doctor?

Depending on the nature of your treatment, your dentist may have specific recommendations about how often you should come in. Typically, adult Invisalign patients will need to visit the dentist at least once every six weeks.
